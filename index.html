A Little More About Me

My name is Nyasia Overton, and as an aspiring software engineer, I am deeply passionate about programming and bringing new ideas to life. My eagerness to continuously learn new concepts is what keeps me motivated.

I love projects that let me express my creative side, like my personal website. Where I built an eye catching, educational webpage dedicated to my favorite manga.
